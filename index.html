About Me

Turning Business Needs Into SAP Solutions

A quick introduction to who I am and what drives my work.

I'm Shihabul Millah, an SAP ABAP Consultant based in Indonesia. My day-to-day work revolves around designing and developing custom solutions on the SAP platform — translating functional requirements into clean, maintainable, and high-performance ABAP code.

From classical and interactive reports (ALV), SmartForms and Adobe Forms, to user exits, BAdIs, and enhancement implementations, I enjoy the full spectrum of ABAP development. I also work on system integrations through BAPIs, RFCs, and IDocs, connecting SAP with the outside world.

Beyond SAP, I'm a web developer and lifelong IT enthusiast — always curious about new technologies and how they can make business processes simpler, faster, and more enjoyable to use.

What I Do

Core services I deliver across the SAP development lifecycle.

ABAP Development

Custom reports (classical, interactive, ALV), module pool programs, and clean, well-structured ABAP code following development best practices.

Enhancements & Exits

Extending SAP standard behavior safely with user exits, customer exits, BAdIs, and the Enhancement Framework — without breaking upgrades.

Forms & Output

Business documents your users will love — SmartForms, Adobe Forms, and SAPscript for invoices, purchase orders, delivery notes, and more.

Integration & Interfaces

Connecting SAP with other systems through BAPIs, RFCs, IDocs, and file-based interfaces — reliable data flows in and out of SAP.

Performance & Support
