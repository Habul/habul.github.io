About Me

Turning Business Needs Into SAP Solutions

A quick introduction to who I am and what drives my work.

I'm Shihabul Millah, an SAP ABAP Consultant based in Tangerang, Indonesia. I'm highly motivated to keep developing my abilities and growing professionally — building applications and taking part in Indonesia's digital advancement.

My day-to-day work covers the full technical side of SAP: HANA views and stored procedures to speed up heavy reports, CDS views feeding SAP Analytics Cloud, Smart Forms and Adobe Forms for business documents, and interfaces built on BAPIs and RFCs that keep SAP in sync with the systems around it. I also translate business requirements into technical specifications and tune programs for runtime and memory efficiency.

Before SAP, I spent a decade keeping retail POS systems and databases running — experience that still shapes how I think about reliability. Outside of SAP I work with Laravel and CodeIgniter, and I'm especially interested in contributing to innovative solutions in the fintech sector that widen financial access for the community.
